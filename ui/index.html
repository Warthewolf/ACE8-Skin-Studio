Prepare // 01

Extract a skin

Pulls an aircraft's textures out of the game files and turns them into tiles you can paint on. The game files themselves are never changed.

Which aircraft

Skin folders to take. Pick any to take only those, or leave them all unpicked to take every skin. Each one is roughly 130 to 320 MB of raw files.

Preview skins shows what each folder looks like before you extract. It fetches only each skin's colour texture, makes a small picture of it, and deletes the full-size file again.

What to take

Extracted skins

Every skin texture the program can see. Raw files from your own earlier extractions can be added too.

Prepare // 03

Prepare a model

A model exported from the game with FModel also carries the cockpit, glass, seat and weapons, each with its own textures. This keeps only the parts that wear the skin, so Substance Painter shows a single texture set.

Drop .glb models here. Add the aircraft, and if you want to paint them too, its door, probe and pylon models (names ending in GearOff, RefuelOff, DPTK or DPP, with or without a number, such as DPP1 or DPP2).

Parts to keep

Materials with "Decal" in the name wear the skin and are ticked for you. The tile count shows how many UDIM tiles a part spreads over.

Repack into a mod

01Skin to replace

Extract the skin first (Extract in the menu), or add a folder that already holds its raw .uasset, .uexp and .ubulk files.

The new skin starts as a copy of this skin: its material, finish, normal map and markings rules. It is the folder's number unless you type another.

02Your painted files

Optional for a new skin slot: without pictures, the new skin looks like the one it is based on and only its name, description and icon are new.

Drop your exported pictures or their folder here. Tiles and whole pictures both work: either one picture per tile with the tile number in its name (1001, 1002 and so on), or one wide picture of the whole skin with no number, which does not need splitting first. The name must also say what the picture is: Substance Painter's names (Base_color, Metallic, Roughness, Normal) or the game's own endings (_C, _MRX, _NO). Aircraft with a picture per part, such as the Ladon (Body and Wing), need the part in the name too. If the folder holds more than one set, you choose which to use below.

New emblem

Add an emblem of your own to the game's emblem list, with its own name and description. It can go on any aircraft, and every emblem of the game stays as it was.

01Picture

Drop the emblem's picture here, or choose it. A PNG with a see-through background works best: the see-through parts show the aircraft's paint. The whole picture is used; it is fitted into a square, never cut.

Manage // 07

Skin slots

New skins in slots of their own and new emblems, from you and from others. Every skin mod here works next to the others: the program keeps one shared skin list and emblem list for all of them and gives each skin and emblem a free number.

Import a skin mod

Drop a skin mod's .zip here (or copy it in Explorer and press Ctrl+V), for example one downloaded from Nexus Mods. Mods made with ACE8 Skin Studio are fitted in next to your other skins, and any skin whose number is taken is given a free one. Other mods are installed as they are; if they bring their own skin list, their skins are taken into the shared one.

Installed skin mods

Other mods with their own skin list

These mods carry their own copy of the game's skin list. Their skins and changes are copied into the shared list, so they keep working next to the skin mods above.

Manage // 08

Installed mods

What is in your mods folder, which game files each mod replaces, and which mods get in each other's way.

Mods

Remove takes a mod out of the mods folder without deleting it. It is moved into the workspace, in the removed-mods folder; move it back to restore it, or delete it there for good. Close the game before removing a mod. Skin mods with new skin slots are best removed on the Skin slots screen, which also updates the skin list.

Manage // 09

Save tools

Unlock your new skins and emblems straight away, get your save ready for the game without mods, and put a backup back. Close the game first. Both save files are backed up before every change, and whenever the skin list changes.

Unlock now

New skins and emblems unlock when you finish a campaign mission, the way the game unlocks its own. This unlocks them in your save straight away instead, marked as new in the hangar.

Going back to the game without mods

Do this before you take the mods out to play the game as it came. It takes the new skins and emblems out of your save: out of the unlocked lists, and off every aircraft wearing one (a skin goes back to the aircraft's first skin, an emblem to none). Your progress, money, aircraft and everything else stay as they are.

When the mods are back, press Unlock now (or finish a mission) and choose them again in the hangar.

Backups

Backups are kept next to the game's SaveGames folder, in SkinStudioSaveBackups, where the game and Steam Cloud leave them alone. Only Campaign.sav is ever changed; the replays are not touched.

Your save is also backed up by itself whenever the skin list changes (a skin or emblem is built, imported, changed or taken out), unless the newest backup already has it as it is. The last 20 of those are kept; backups of every other kind are kept until you delete them.

Help // 10

Useful links

Other modders' pages with templates for the game's skins: paint your skin on a template, then bring it into the game with Repack or New skin slot. They open in your browser; all credit goes to their authors.

01Using a template with Skin Studio

  1. Download the template for your aircraft from its page (Nexus Mods may ask you to sign in).
  2. Paint on it in Photoshop, GIMP or Substance Painter, and export your pictures as PNG or TGA, one per tile (1001, 1002 and so on), as the template explains.
  3. Use Repack to replace a game skin with yours, or New skin slot to add it as an extra skin. Name your files after the texture (for example T_PP0021_su57_001_C_1001.png) so the program knows what each picture is for, or tell it on the screen.

About

Mods made here replace or add game files, so the game needs a mod loader that accepts them; new skin slots need AC8 Mod Loader 1.1.0 or newer. Use modded files offline and in single player only.

The report holds the version, these settings and the last task's log, ready to paste to whoever is helping you. The AES key is never included, and your Windows user name is taken out of the folder paths.
